## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS in `src/`, built by `scripts/build-site.sh` into `_site/` and deployed to GitHub Pages. Contact values (WhatsApp, email) are injected at build time.

## What it is

Log N Labs is a small team in Asunción, Paraguay that does software, marketing and consulting for small businesses (pymes). Source: docs/ESTRATEGIA.md, docs/CONTENIDO.md.

## Users

Owners of Paraguayan pymes with 1 to 20 employees who sell through WhatsApp, Instagram/Facebook and a physical store, mostly in Gran Asunción and Central. The owner decides. Typical sectors: clothing and cosmetics, appointment-based services (clinics, workshops, beauty), hardware, spare parts and food shops.

## Position

The same team builds the brand, runs the campaigns and implements the software. Campaigns are reported in guaraníes per sale, not in reach or likes.

## Lines of work

- **Software:** general software implementations: custom systems, integrations between existing tools, automations, websites, QA and audits. Do not present electronic invoicing (factura electrónica, SIFEN) or any specific third-party service (QR payments, WhatsApp Business, named providers) as something we offer. (Owner decision, 2026-11-18.)
- **Marketing:** strategy, visual identity, Meta and Google campaigns with sales tracking and a monthly report. Ad spend is paid by the client directly to the platform.
- **Consultoría:** diagnosis of how the business sells, collects and invoices, with a written plan.

## Voice

Neutral Spanish addressed as tú, no voseo. Loss-based main message ("Que no se te escape ninguna venta") that always names the concrete loss. Plain shop-owner words. No inflated verbs, empty adjectives, agency jargon, exclamation marks, fake scarcity or em dashes. Full rules: docs/CONTENIDO.md §1.

## Evidence and constraints

- Values in `[brackets]` are owner TODOs; never invent them.
